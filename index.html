About SK Events

Transforming moments into memories

Founded in 2004, SK Events is a Hyderabad-based event planning and decoration company renowned for transforming ordinary moments into extraordinary memories. From weddings and birthdays to corporate events and cultural celebrations, our team delivers customized event solutions tailored to each client's dream.

With more than two decades of experience, a reputation for innovation, and a 4.5+ star client rating, SK Events continues to set the standard in premium event management. Every celebration we organize is a unique reflection of our client's personality, style, and joy.

Our Services

Comprehensive event solutions for every occasion

Weddings

We offer end-to-end wedding planning and decoration, including engagement ceremonies, Haldi, Mehendi, sangeet, wedding day decor, receptions, bridal showers, and more. From traditional mandaps to modern themes, every detail is handled with precision and creativity.

Birthdays
